# Agricultural Insights & Decision-Support Architecture

## 1. Multi-Tier Insights Framework

To ensure transparent, defensible, and responsible AI recommendations, YieldSense AI strictly separates its analytical outputs into three distinct levels of authority:

1. **MODEL PREDICTION**: Quantitative estimations produced directly by trained machine learning models with measurable statistical bounds.
2. **DATA-DRIVEN INSIGHT**: Empirical observations calculated from historical datasets (e.g., soil pH suitability, crop-texture yields, and weather envelopes).
3. **GENERAL AGRICULTURAL GUIDANCE**: Agronomic best practices and domain guidelines for soil conservation, irrigation management, and crop rotation.
